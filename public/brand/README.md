# MB AI Group · Dark Glass

Approved direction: dark cinematic glass + shared-stem MB (Logo 2).

- **Mark:** `mbai-mark.svg`, hand-authored continuous silhouette, transparent background. The M diagonal flows into the B's crown, the B vertical forms the shared stem. White/off-white only; no badge or container in navigation. `components/Logo.tsx` mirrors the SVG path.
- **Wordmark:** MB AI Group, Inter Medium, compact tracking. Optional Cairo · Dubai descriptor.
- **Palette:** backdrop #080D0F; glass #11191C; foreground #F1F5F4; secondary #A7B7B4; accent #2A9D8F. Teal CTA surface #246F69 for white-label contrast. Thin pale-teal/white borders, not colored outlines.
- **Surfaces:** translucent charcoal gradient, 24px backdrop blur, 1px low-opacity white border, restrained top sheen and soft black shadow.
- **Typography:** self-hosted Inter Variable (100–900). One family for display, body and labels. System sans fallback for Arabic glyphs.
- **Imagery:** real product captures and original product thumbnails retain their own colors. Marketing chrome remains neutral/teal. Never manufacture product screens or use invented usage metrics.
- **Hero:** overlapping glass browser/device frames with TUT's real desktop capture and real IBNI / Virlo Studio home-page captures. No stock portrait grid or generic name cards.
- **Motion:** no floating-device loop. Slideshow supports pause, focus/hover pause and prefers-reduced-motion. Native navigation, no scroll hijack.

Favicons, touch/maskable icons and Open Graph assets share the same master silhouette. Icon backgrounds are plain near-black for OS legibility; the logo itself has no tile.
